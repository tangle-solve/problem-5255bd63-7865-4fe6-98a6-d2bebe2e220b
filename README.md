### CHEM-000083: Closing organofluorine mass balances in complex products
- **Subfield:** Analytical chemistry and molecular identification
- **Open:** Reconcile total, extractable, inorganic, polymeric and individually identified fluorine in realistic products and environmental samples within validated uncertainties.
- **Status:** Targeted PFAS assays leave unidentified fractions. A 2018 cosmetics study demonstrated substantial mass-balance gaps; general analytical closure remains open.
- **Type:** experimental | data
- **Why:** Prevents targeted testing from overlooking major fluorinated reservoirs.
- **Key works:** Schultes et al. (2018), *Per- and polyfluoroalkyl substances and fluorine mass balance in cosmetic products from the Swedish market: implications for environmental emissions and human exposure*, [DOI:10.1039/C8EM00368H](https://doi.org/10.1039/C8EM00368H).
- **Related:** Environmental science: inverse source apportionment.
